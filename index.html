    Frequently Asked Questions

    How much does a mobile app cost?

    The cost depends on the scope, features, and complexity of your app. A focused MVP with core functionality typically starts at a fraction of what hiring a full-time developer costs for a year. After a free consultation I'll give you a clear quote — no vague estimates, no surprise invoices. You'll know the price before we start.

    How long does it take to build an app?

    Most projects are delivered in 4 to 8 weeks. A focused MVP can be ready even faster. The timeline depends on scope — the clearer your requirements, the faster we move. I'll give you a realistic schedule upfront and stick to it. No endless delays, no moving goalposts.

    Do I need to prepare technical specs?

    Not at all. You just need an idea and a problem you want to solve for your customers. I'll ask the right questions, help shape the product vision, and translate your business goals into a concrete app plan. No tech knowledge required on your side — that's exactly what you're hiring me for.

    What if I need changes or new features after launch?

    Apps evolve as your business grows — that's completely normal. I offer ongoing support and development packages so you can add features, fix issues, or update the app as needed. You won't be left with a product you can't update. Everything is built to be maintainable and extendable from day one.

    How do we start working together?

    Simple. Click "Start your project", fill in a short form describing your idea, and we'll schedule a free 30-minute call to align on goals and scope. After the call you receive a proposal with timeline and pricing. If it works for you — we start. No commitment required until you're ready.
